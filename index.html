ESL Curriculum Dashboard

A Culturally Responsive C1 English Curriculum for Sudanese Women

This interactive dashboard provides a comprehensive overview of the 7-week, culturally responsive English language course. The curriculum is designed to create a supportive and collaborative learning environment, valuing the learners' cultural backgrounds as a foundational asset for acquiring advanced C1-level proficiency.

Course Vision

To extend beyond linguistic instruction, fostering a supportive environment where participants explore identities, share experiences, and build practical skills for their personal and professional futures.

Pedagogical Framework

Benchmarked at a "toned-down C1" level, focusing on practical application of high-level skills. The approach is learner-centered, using authentic contexts to build confidence and strategic competence.

Task-Based Learning

Employs a task-based methodology where language is a tool to accomplish meaningful goals. This integrates reading, writing, listening, and speaking into cohesive, real-world activities.

Weekly Skills Progression

The curriculum is carefully scaffolded, moving from personal and internal themes to broader societal and professional engagement. This chart visualizes the shifting focus on different skill domains each week, demonstrating a balanced and progressive learning journey.
